# Interface copy

Every word the app shows that isn't a question or a blurb. Edit freely — none of
it is referenced by the scoring code, only looked up by the heading above it.

`{name}`, `{tagline}`, `{n}` and `{total}` are filled in at runtime. Keep them
spelled exactly as they are; anything else is just text.

A blank line inside a block starts a new paragraph, and a single line break is
kept as a line break — which is how the title breaks across two lines.

`crab.lines` is the exception: one line per thing the crab in the corner says.
It works through them in order and starts again from the top.
